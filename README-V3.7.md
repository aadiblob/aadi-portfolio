# Portfolio V3.7 — Animated Supercharger Exploded View

This root-flat patch replaces the former component-scope row with one black-and-white **Explode** control.

## Interaction

- Clicking **Explode** slides the project copy off the left edge.
- The viewer expands into the center of the section.
- The camera returns to the approved three-quarter hero orientation.
- The housing lifts and the 20 remaining Onshape occurrences separate in a staged mechanical sequence.
- The exploded assembly remains rotatable and zoomable after the transition.
- The button changes to **Assemble** and reverses the complete sequence.
